PalSafar Account & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How PalSafar collects, uses, processes, stores, discloses, transfers, and safeguards your Personal Data when you use our Platform.

Your data. Your choice. Last Updated: 15 August 2026

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your Personal Data.

We at PalSafar (“PalSafar”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) value your privacy and are committed to protecting your Personal Data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, transfer, and safeguard your Personal Data when you access or use our website, mobile application, or any other products, services, or features offered by PalSafar (collectively referred to as the “Platform”).

PalSafar is a technology platform that enables users to discover travel destinations, connect with like-minded travellers, explore travel experiences, communicate with fellow users, participate in trips, and access various travel-related services offered directly by PalSafar or through third-party service providers.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide transparency regarding our data processing practices and has been prepared in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and other applicable laws.

By accessing or using the Platform, creating an account, or otherwise providing your Personal Data, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and, where required under applicable law, consent to the collection and processing of your Personal Data in accordance with its terms.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all Personal Data collected, received, processed, stored, used, shared, or otherwise handled by PalSafar in connection with your access to and use of the Platform and the Services. It governs the processing of Personal Data collected through the PalSafar website, mobile application, user registration, profile creation, trip planning and bookings, interactions with other users, customer support, feedback, promotional activities, and all other features or services offered by PalSafar that reference this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy applies irrespective of the device or technology used to access the Platform.

However, it does not apply to third-party websites, applications, payment gateways, social media platforms, or other external services that may be linked to or integrated with the Platform, as such services are governed by their respective privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of such third parties before sharing their Personal Data.

  • Account means the registered account created by a User to access and use the services available on the Platform.
  • Applicable Law means all laws, statutes, regulations, notifications, guidelines, governmental orders, judicial decisions, and regulatory requirements applicable to PalSafar, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Consent means a free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous indication of the User’s agreement to the processing of Personal Data for specified purposes.
  • Device Information means information relating to the device used to access the Platform, including hardware identifiers, operating system, browser type, IP address, device model, language preferences, application version, and network information.
  • Information means any data or content, whether personal or non-personal, provided by a User or otherwise collected by PalSafar in connection with the use of the Platform or the Services.
  • Personal Data means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data and shall have the meaning assigned under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Platform means the PalSafar website, mobile application, and all related digital interfaces, features, functionalities, content, products, and services owned, operated, or made available by PalSafar.
  • Processing includes the collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, sharing, disclosure, transfer, deletion, or any other operation performed on Personal Data.
  • Services means all features, functionalities, travel-related services, communication tools, booking services, community features, recommendations, and any other facilities made available through the Platform.
  • Third Party means any individual, organization, service provider, business partner, government authority, or entity other than PalSafar and the User.
  • User, “you”, or “your” means any individual accessing, browsing, registering on, or using the Platform.

3. Information We Collect

PalSafar may collect and process Personal Data necessary for providing, operating, and improving the Services. Information is collected during account registration, profile creation, trip bookings, communications, customer support interactions, payments, feedback, surveys, and other activities. Such information may include your name, contact details, date of birth, profile information, travel preferences, trip details, payment and billing information, reviews, ratings, and any other Information you choose to provide.

We may also automatically collect certain technical and usage-related Information, including your IP address, device and browser information, operating system, application version, log data, browsing activity, and approximate or precise location information. In addition to the above information, we may receive Information from third-party service providers, payment gateways, analytics providers, business partners, and social media platforms, where permitted by applicable law.

PalSafar may also retain communications exchanged with customer support, responses to surveys or promotional campaigns, complaints, feedback, and other correspondence for quality assurance, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance, and service improvement. Additionally, the Platform may utilise cookies, software development kits (SDKs), web beacons, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, analyse Platform usage, enhance security, and provide a personalized user experience. Further details regarding such technologies are available in PalSafar’s Cookie Policy.

4. How We Collect Information

PalSafar collects Personal Data through lawful, fair, and transparent means. Information may be collected directly from Users when they register an account, use the Platform, complete the profile, book trips, communicate with other users, contact customer support, make payments, submit feedback, or otherwise interact with the Services. We also collect Information automatically through cookies, log files, and similar technologies, as well as through device permissions that you choose to grant.

All Information is collected and processed only to the extent necessary for providing the Services, improving the functionality and security of the Platform, complying with applicable legal obligations, and fulfilling the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

5. Purpose of Processing Personal Data

PalSafar processes your Personal Data only for lawful, specific, and legitimate purposes necessary to provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Platform and the Services. This includes creating, verifying, and managing your account; facilitating trip planning, bookings, travel companion matching, and related travel services; enabling communication between users; processing payments and refunds; providing customer support; sending service-related updates, notifications, and promotional communications, where permitted by applicable law; personalizing your experience; improving the functionality, security, and performance of the Platform; detecting and preventing fraud, misuse, and other unlawful activities; complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements; enforcing our Terms of Use and other policies; and fulfilling any other purpose for which your Personal Data has been collected or as otherwise permitted or required under applicable law.

PalSafar shall not process Personal Data for any purpose that is inconsistent with this Privacy Policy unless such processing is expressly permitted under applicable law or carried out with the User’s prior consent.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosures

PalSafar does not sell or rent your Personal Data to third parties and ensures that recipients process such Personal Data only for authorized purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

PalSafar may share or disclose your Personal Data with its affiliates, service providers, business partners, payment gateways, technology providers, professional advisors, and competent governmental or regulatory authorities where necessary to provide the Services, comply with applicable laws, enforce its legal rights, or protect the security and integrity of the Platform. Where required, limited Personal Data may also be shared with other users to facilitate the Services.

7. Payment Gateway

PalSafar uses authorised and reputable third-party payment gateways and payment service providers to facilitate payments made by Users through the Platform. Depending upon the nature of the transaction, Users may be required to provide certain payment-related information, including information relating to debit cards, credit cards, UPI, internet banking, wallets, or other permitted payment methods.

Payment transactions are processed directly by the relevant payment gateway or payment service provider in accordance with its applicable terms of use, privacy policy, security practices, and applicable laws and regulations. PalSafar does not generally store complete payment credentials, including full debit or credit card credentials, or internet banking credentials, on its own systems.

Where payment-related information is processed by PalSafar for transaction reconciliation, refunds, settlement, fraud prevention, or customer support, such information shall be handled in accordance with applicable law and appropriate security safeguards.

PalSafar shall not be responsible for any unauthorised access, security incident, failure, interruption, or misuse occurring exclusively within the systems of a third-party payment service provider. Users are advised to review the applicable privacy policy and terms of the relevant payment gateway before completing a transaction.

Any refund, reversal, chargeback, failed transaction, or payment dispute may be subject to the policies and processing timelines of the relevant payment gateway, bank, card network, or other financial institution, in addition to PalSafar’s applicable refund and cancellation policies.

8. Data Retention

PalSafar shall retain Personal Data only for such period as is reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which such Personal Data was collected or otherwise processed. The retention period may vary depending upon the nature and sensitivity of the Personal Data, the purpose of processing, the nature of the Services availed, the User’s relationship with PalSafar, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

PalSafar may retain Personal Data for purposes including providing and maintaining the Services, maintaining transaction and booking records, processing payments and refunds, resolving complaints and disputes, preventing fraud and misuse, enforcing contractual rights, maintaining business and financial records, complying with tax, accounting, regulatory and reporting obligations, and responding to lawful requests from governmental, judicial, or regulatory authorities.

Where Personal Data is no longer required for the purposes for which it was collected or where the applicable retention period has expired, PalSafar shall take reasonable steps to securely delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of such Personal Data in accordance with applicable law. Where deletion is not immediately possible due to technical, legal, regulatory, evidentiary, or backup-related requirements, PalSafar may retain the relevant information for such additional period as may be reasonably necessary, after which it shall be deleted in accordance with applicable law.

Further detail on typical retention periods after account deletion is set out on our Account & Data Deletion page.

PalSafar implements reasonable technical, organisational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction, or other forms of misuse.

Depending upon the nature of the information and the risks associated with its processing, such safeguards may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption or other appropriate security technologies, secure servers and infrastructure, network security measures, data backups, monitoring mechanisms, vulnerability assessments, periodic security reviews, incident management procedures, and confidentiality obligations applicable to employees, contractors, and authorised service providers.

Access to Personal Data shall be restricted to persons who require such access for legitimate business, operational, contractual, legal, or service-related purposes. PalSafar may periodically review and update its security practices to address technological developments, operational requirements, and identified security risks.

Notwithstanding the safeguards implemented by PalSafar, no electronic transmission, storage system, website, application, or method of communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and should promptly notify PalSafar of any suspected unauthorised access or compromise of their account.

16. User Rights

Subject to the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and other applicable laws, Users, in their capacity as Data Principals, shall be entitled to exercise the following rights in relation to their Personal Data processed by PalSafar:

  • Right to Access Information To obtain information relating to the Personal Data processed by PalSafar, including details regarding such processing, in accordance with applicable law.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure To request the correction, completion, updating, or erasure of Personal Data that is inaccurate, incomplete, or no longer required, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent To withdraw previously granted consent for the processing of Personal Data at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing undertaken prior to such withdrawal and subject to any legal or contractual obligations.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal To raise complaints or grievances concerning the processing of Personal Data or any alleged non-compliance with applicable data protection laws by contacting PalSafar’s designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate To nominate another individual to exercise the rights of the User in accordance with the provisions of the DPDP Act.

Users may exercise the aforesaid rights by submitting a request through the contact details specified in this Privacy Policy. PalSafar reserves the right to verify the identity and authority of the requester and may seek such additional information as may be reasonably necessary before processing any request. All requests shall be addressed in accordance with the provisions of the DPDP Act and other applicable laws.
