✦ FAQS ✦

Questions? Here are Answers.

Everything you need to know about PalSafar, PalPoints, rewards, bookings and more.

?

General

What is PalSafar?⌄

PalSafar is a travel platform that helps you plan smarter with AI, discover hidden gems, watch travel reels, and earn PalPoints as you explore.

How does PalSafar work?⌄

Create a trip, explore the smart map, save places, watch local reels, and redeem rewards with partner businesses — all in one app.

Is PalSafar free to use?⌄

Yes. PalSafar is free for travellers. Premium tools are available for creators and businesses.

What makes PalSafar different?⌄

PalSafar combines AI trip planning, a smart explore map, travel reels, and PalPoints rewards in one place built for India.

Can I plan my entire trip on PalSafar?⌄

Yes. Use the AI Trip Planner to build a personalised itinerary, then discover places, stays and local experiences as you go.

Can I compare and book rides through PalSafar?⌄

Ride comparison and booking tools are part of the PalSafar roadmap so you can move between destinations with less friction.

🎁

Rewards & PalPoints

What are PalPoints?⌄

PalPoints are PalSafar’s reward currency. Earn them by exploring, checking in, sharing reels, and completing activities.

How do I earn PalPoints?⌄

Earn points by visiting places, sharing travel reels, writing reviews, and completing in-app challenges.

Where can I use PalPoints?⌄

Use PalPoints with verified local businesses, experiences, and exclusive PalSafar partner offers.

How can I redeem my points?⌄

Redeem PalPoints for exclusive offers, discounts, and local experiences from verified businesses.

Do PalPoints expire?⌄

PalPoints typically do not expire while your account stays active. See the Rewards Policy for full details.

Are there limits on reward redemptions?⌄

Some partner offers have daily or campaign limits. Each reward card shows its remaining availability.

🏪

Creators & Businesses

How can businesses join PalSafar?⌄

Register as a vendor to get a business profile, appear on the explore map, and run offers.

What are the benefits for businesses?⌄

Get discovered by travellers already exploring nearby, run campaigns, and track views, interactions and redemptions.

How does business listing work?⌄

Verified businesses show up where travellers are exploring — on the smart map, place cards, and local offers.

What are the pricing plans for businesses?⌄

Basic listings are free. Premium visibility and campaign tools will be available on flexible plans.

How can I contact PalSafar support?⌄

Email support@palsafar.in or reach us on Instagram and WhatsApp. The Team page also lists how to get in touch.

How can creators earn rewards?⌄

Creators earn PalPoints, unlock collaborations, and grow an audience of travellers actively exploring.
