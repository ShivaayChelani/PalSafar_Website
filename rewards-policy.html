Rewards Policy

This Policy governs participation in PalSafar Rewards and the earning and redemption of PalPoints.

PalPoints. Your rewards. Last Updated: 15 August 2026

Participation in PalSafar Rewards is voluntary.

This Rewards Policy (“Policy”) governs the participation of Users in the rewards and loyalty programme operated under the name “PalSafar Rewards” (“Rewards Programme”) by Rahul Chelani, a sole proprietor carrying on business under the trade name “PalSafar” (“PalSafar”, “Proprietor”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

The Rewards Programme is intended to provide eligible Users with opportunities to earn PalPoints through specified travel-related activities, engagement with the Platform, sharing of experiences, completion of challenges, participation in promotional campaigns, and such other activities as may be identified by PalSafar from time to time. Accumulated PalPoints may, subject to the applicable conditions, be redeemed for rewards made available under the Rewards Programme.

Participation in the Rewards Programme is voluntary. By registering for, accessing, or participating in the Rewards Programme, the User acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to this Policy, the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and other applicable policies of PalSafar.

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, unless the context otherwise requires:

  • Account means the User account created on the Platform through which the User may access the Rewards Programme and maintain their PalPoints balance.
  • PalPoints means the reward points or loyalty points credited to an eligible User for completing Qualifying Activities under the Rewards Programme.
  • Platform means the website, mobile application, digital interface, or other online medium operated or made available by PalSafar through which Users may access the Services and Rewards Programme.
  • Qualifying Activity means any activity designated by PalSafar as eligible for earning PalPoints, including travel activities, sharing experiences, completing challenges, participating in campaigns, making eligible transactions, referrals, or other activities notified by PalSafar.
  • Reward means any product, merchandise, voucher, travel benefit, experience, gift, electronic device, vehicle, or other benefit made available for redemption through the Rewards Programme.
  • Rewards Catalogue means the list of Rewards and corresponding PalPoint redemption requirements displayed by PalSafar from time to time.
  • Rewards Programme means the PalSafar loyalty and rewards programme under which Users may earn and redeem PalPoints.
  • User means an individual who has registered on or otherwise uses the Platform and participates in the Rewards Programme.

2. Eligibility

Participation in the Rewards Programme may be made available to Users who:

  1. have a valid Account with PalSafar;
  2. provide accurate and complete registration information;
  3. satisfy any age, geographical, verification, or other eligibility requirements specified by PalSafar;
  4. comply with the Terms & Conditions and this Policy; and
  5. are not suspended, restricted, or otherwise prohibited from participating in the Rewards Programme.

PalSafar may prescribe different eligibility requirements for particular campaigns, challenges, Rewards, or promotional activities.

PalSafar reserves the right to verify the eligibility of any User at any time. Where a User fails to satisfy applicable eligibility requirements, PalSafar may refuse participation, withhold PalPoints, cancel pending redemptions, or suspend the User’s participation in the Rewards Programme.

3. Earning PalPoints

Users may earn PalPoints by completing Qualifying Activities identified by PalSafar from time to time. Such activities may include, without limitation, exploring or visiting destinations, sharing genuine travel experiences, uploading eligible content, completing challenges, participating in campaigns, making eligible bookings, providing feedback, referring users, or undertaking other activities announced by PalSafar.

The number of PalPoints awarded for each Qualifying Activity shall be determined by PalSafar and may vary depending upon the nature of the activity, promotional campaign, User eligibility, booking value, destination, or other applicable criteria. PalSafar shall have the right to introduce, modify, suspend, or discontinue any method of earning PalPoints at its discretion.

PalPoints shall be credited only after PalSafar verifies that the relevant Qualifying Activity has been validly completed. PalSafar may withhold or delay the crediting of PalPoints where verification is pending or where the activity is subject to cancellation, refund, dispute, fraud review, or other verification requirements.

4. Content & Experience-Based Rewards

Where PalPoints are awarded for sharing travel experiences, photographs, videos, reviews, stories, recommendations, or other content, the User shall ensure that such content is genuine, lawful, accurate, and does not infringe the rights of any third party.

PalSafar may review submitted content before awarding PalPoints and may reject content that is misleading, repetitive, inappropriate, fraudulent, offensive, unlawful, promotional without authorisation, or otherwise inconsistent with the requirements of the Rewards Programme. Submission of content shall not automatically guarantee the award of PalPoints.

5. Challenges & Promotional Campaigns

PalSafar may periodically introduce challenges, contests, campaigns, missions, promotional activities, or special earning opportunities through which Users may earn additional PalPoints or other benefits.

Each such activity may have separate eligibility requirements, validity periods, completion criteria, and PalPoint allocations. Users shall be required to satisfy all applicable conditions within the specified period to qualify for the relevant reward.

PalSafar reserves the right to modify, suspend, extend, or discontinue any challenge or promotional campaign at any time, subject to applicable law.

6. PalPoint Account

PalPoints earned by a User shall be reflected in the User’s account on the Platform. Users shall be responsible for regularly reviewing their PalPoint balance and transaction history and shall promptly notify PalSafar of any apparent error or unauthorised activity.

PalPoints are promotional reward units provided by PalSafar solely for participation in the Rewards Programme and do not constitute money, electronic money, a bank deposit, security, or any other monetary instrument. PalPoints shall not accrue interest and shall have no cash value except to the extent expressly provided under the Rewards Programme.

7. Redemption of PalPoints

Eligible Users may redeem their accumulated PalPoints for Rewards made available by PalSafar from time to time. The number of PalPoints required for a particular Reward shall be determined by PalSafar and may vary depending upon the Reward, availability, promotional period, or other applicable conditions.

For example, the Rewards Programme may make available premium products or experiences against specified PalPoint thresholds, including rewards such as electronic devices, travel-related benefits, lifestyle products, or other merchandise.

Redemption shall be subject to the User having sufficient PalPoints and satisfying all applicable eligibility and verification requirements.

PalPoints are promotional loyalty points issued by PalSafar solely for participation in the Rewards Programme and shall not constitute cash, currency, electronic money, a deposit, security, or any other monetary or financial instrument. PalPoints shall not be sold, purchased, pledged, exchanged, or otherwise dealt with for monetary consideration or any other unauthorised benefit. PalPoints may be used only by the eligible User, or by a recipient User where PalPoints have been validly transferred through the authorised transfer mechanism, for redemption against Rewards made available through the Rewards Programme and in accordance with the applicable redemption requirements.

PalPoints earned by a User shall not expire and shall remain valid indefinitely, unless such PalPoints are cancelled, reversed, or adjusted in accordance with this Rewards Policy, applicable campaign terms, or applicable law.

PalPoints may be cancelled, reversed, or adjusted where they have been awarded incorrectly, obtained through fraud, misuse, unauthorised activity, cancellation or reversal of an eligible transaction, or in any other circumstances expressly permitted under this Rewards Policy or applicable law. Any PalPoints so cancelled, reversed, or adjusted shall no longer be available for redemption, and the User shall not be entitled to any cash, refund, compensation, or other monetary benefit in respect of such PalPoints.

For the avoidance of doubt, PalPoints shall not be subject to any general expiry period, and PalSafar shall not impose an expiry date on validly earned PalPoints merely by reason of the passage of time.

Once PalPoints have been redeemed for a Reward, the redemption request shall ordinarily be considered final and shall not be eligible for cancellation, return, exchange, or refund, except where expressly permitted by PalSafar or required under applicable law. PalPoints shall not be refundable or convertible into cash merely because a User chooses not to use them or is unable to redeem them for a particular Reward.

In the event that PalSafar is unable to fulfil a confirmed Reward redemption due to unavailability, discontinuation, or any other reason attributable to PalSafar, PalSafar may, at its discretion and subject to applicable law, offer the User an alternative Reward of comparable value, reinstate the PalPoints used for the redemption, or provide such other resolution as may be reasonably appropriate.
